# Ember-2 v0.10.0 — She Thinks Out Loud Now

**Released:** 2026-03-27

Ember-2 is a local, private personal intelligence system. Your memories, your hardware, your rules.

## What's New

**Streaming.** Ember used to think in silence for 10-20 seconds, then speak all at once. Now she thinks out loud — the first word appears in 1-2 seconds and the response builds in real time. Like watching someone think, not waiting for an email.

**She notices what matters.** Ember now automatically detects when you mention a project you're working on, something blocking you, a goal, or an open loop — and remembers it as operational state. You don't have to tell her "remember this." She picks it up from the conversation.

**Project context.** When you're working inside a project, Ember prefers memories from that project. Not a filter — a boost. She still sees everything, but project-relevant memories surface first.

**She has opinions now.** A new constitutional principle — authentic_expression — means Ember no longer deflects when asked what she thinks. "I don't have personal preferences" is gone. She answers directly.

**Performance.** Vector indexes are cached in memory (2-4 seconds faster per query). Buffer compression runs in the background. The response pipeline is the fastest it's ever been.

**Install from your phone.** PWA support means you can add Ember to your home screen on Android or iOS. One tap to open. No app store.

**Vault health.** `python scripts/audit_memory.py` gives you a full diagnostic — record counts, schema validation, duplicate detection, junk flagging, index health, and an overall GREEN/YELLOW/RED score.

**196 tests passing.** 43 new this release.

## Technical Details

### Major Features
- SSE streaming via `ollama.chat(stream=True)` through FastAPI `StreamingResponse`
- Auto state extraction via `StateExtractor` — background LLM call per turn
- Project-scoped retrieval boost (ADR-007) — +0.15 for matching `project_id`
- Cloud model provider architecture planned (ADR-008) — pending acceptance

### Performance
- Vector index in-memory caching — module-level dict, auto-invalidation on write
- Buffer compression moved to background thread

### Architecture
- Typed memory enforcement — `VALID_MEMORY_TYPES` in `storage.py`, 17 types
- Retrieval eval expanded to 15 benchmark cases with pass/warn/fail scoring
- Vault health audit script with 7 checks
- Constitutional principle: `authentic_expression`
